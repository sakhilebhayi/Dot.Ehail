# R-46 accessibility and mobile usability (a11y agent, wave 3)

Branch `feat/r46-a11y`. Scope: every passenger, driver, operations, business, fleet and public page, and the live
states of a real ride. Decisions D-1700 to D-1717 in `R46_DECISIONS.md`; skills record in `R46_SKILLS.md`. Method
reused from R-45 (Dot.Design): axe-core sweep, keyboard walk, responsive, motion, focus obscured.

## 1. Method

All checks run in real Chrome (the installed Google Chrome through `playwright-core`, headless), against the dev
server with the integrated backend. Nothing is mocked except routing and geocoding (a local OSRM and Nominatim
stand-in, `geo-stub.mjs`) and map tiles (blocked, never fetched by automation). Dev only; see D-1700, D-1701.

```sh
npm run build && php scripts/dev/a11y/fixtures.php     # people and reference data (refuses production)
sh scripts/dev/a11y/serve.sh &                         # dev server on 8623 + geo stand-in on 8693, cash on
sh scripts/dev/a11y/run-all.sh                         # every check below; logs and JSON in storage/a11y/
```

| Script | What it checks |
|---|---|
| `sweep.mjs` | axe-core 4.14.0, tags wcag2a, wcag2aa, wcag21a, wcag21aa, wcag22aa, best-practice; every page and live state at 390, 768 and 1440 px, light and night; also horizontal overflow, h1 count and duplicate ids. `--ci` fails on any serious or critical rule. |
| `keyboard.mjs` | Real key events. Pages: skip link first and working, Tab walk ends or wraps without a trap, every stop named, every stop with a visible focus ring, no focused control hidden under the sticky bars (WCAG 2.4.11), no invisible stops. | `mobile.mjs` | Every page at 320, 360, 390, 430 px and landscape 844x390: no horizontal scroll; at 390: touch targets 44 px measured by hit-testing (what a tap reaches, so pseudo-element extensions and labels count), radio and checkbox rows 44 px, driver primary actions 56 px, no backdrop blur or blur filters; text at 200% (root size doubled) without horizontal scroll; the virtual keyboard (viewport shortened to 55%) never covering the focused field (place search, help case, sign in, business booking, driver application); the offline notice on booking. |

## 3. What was fixed

Screen readers and keyboard:
- Account menu named by its words only; the avatar's initials are drawn (D-1709).
- Passenger PIN has a valid name (`role="img"`, "Trip PIN 7 2 7 4").
- Driver offer and driver SOS are modal (background inert, focus on Accept or Cancel, focus returned afterwards);
  Escape stops a counting SOS; the countdown is announced at 10 and 5 s; PIN progress in words (D-1703).
- Announcements without spam (D-1702): the booking price clock was read every second (inside a live region); the ops
  map status re-spoke every 5 s; the driver alert box and status line re-spoke on every poll. Now: the price clock is
  a timer read only on request and announced once at one minute left, option lists are announced as one sentence,
  the passenger hears the arrival time when the minute changes (at most once a minute, at once at two minutes),
  the ops board says when a queue goes up.
- The map in words for the live ride (stops, driver distance, arrival, stale position) (D-1704); ops queues already
  list what the ops map shows.
- Valid lists (ops and fleet figures), named scrollable tables, named action columns, legal page h1, heading order on
  Jetstream pages and the legacy dashboard, unique driver status region, legacy notification bell names its count.
- Focus never hidden: scroll padding for the sticky bars; the booking sheet opens when focus enters its hidden part;
  the map attribution and the map banner no longer cover controls (D-1708, D-1713).
- Focus rings for file and date inputs (Chrome does not match `:focus-visible` on them).

Contrast and sunlight:
- Map attribution readable in the night theme (Leaflet's white panel was winning) and placed top right (D-1708).
- Legacy dashboard greys lifted; the legacy bell badge darker.
- Driver light theme at AAA 7:1 (links, go badges, switch states, SOS button, the online status bar) (D-1716).

Mobile:
- 44 px targets on phones and touch screens across the kit and the portals (D-1712); driver primaries were already
  56 to 96 px.
- Safe areas: notch adds to the top bar; side insets in landscape (D-1711).
- Driver Go online and Go offline lower on the phone (D-1717).
- Reduced motion reaches every map caller (D-1705).

Copy: no en or em dashes in visible text; one name per ride category on every surface (D-1710); fallback errors
that say what to do (D-1714, D-1715).

## 4. Measured mobile facts

- No page scrolls sideways at 320, 360, 390, 430 px or in landscape, and none at 390 with text at 200%.
- Driver console at 390x844 after the fix: Go online spans y 354 to 450 (was 284 to 380), today's figure below it,
  the tab bar at 772. The trip view keeps its order (route, next stop, then the stage action): on a short trip screen
  the stage action is in the upper half; it is 72 to 96 px tall. Not changed (driver agent's layout; recorded).
- Passenger booking: the request bar is the last block of the sheet, at the bottom when the sheet is open; the sheet
  handle is 44 px.
- Virtual keyboard: place search, help case, sign in, business booking and driver application fields stay visible
  with the viewport shortened to 55%. The driver PIN pad has no text field (no keyboard opens).
- No backdrop blur or blur filter on any page; shadows only on floating layers (unchanged).
- Offline: the booking page shows "You are offline" in the sheet; the live ride shows the feed's connection notice
  (driven by the sweep's journey, not by forcing offline).

## 6. Limits, honestly

- No real screen reader was run (no VoiceOver, TalkBack or NVDA session). Names, roles, live regions and focus were
  verified through the accessibility tree, axe and scripted key events; how a real reader voices and queues them is
  not verified.
- No physical phone. Phones were emulated in desktop Chrome (viewport, touch emulation, `isMobile` off); the virtual
  keyboard was simulated by shortening the viewport; safe-area insets are zero in emulation, so D-1711 is a CSS
  change verified by reading, not by a notched device. Safari and Firefox were not run.
- 200% text was simulated by doubling the root font size (the product's sizes are rem-based); browser zoom and the
  Android font-size setting were not run.
- Map tiles were blocked, so the map's own drawing (roads, labels) was not judged; the attribution, controls, markers
  and banners were.
- The offer lives about 15 s, so the sweep judges it at 390 night (first ride) and 390 and 1440 light (second ride),
  not at 768 or 1440 night; `sunlight.mjs` judges it again in daylight. The SOS countdown (3 s) once per app.
- The 15 s offer window is a real-time limit (WCAG 2.2.1 real-time exception); it is not adjustable by the driver.
- Low-end performance was judged by inspection (no blur, no heavy effects) and the existing perf budgets, not on a
  low-end device.
- Copy check covered dashes, category names and error fallbacks; a full plain-language edit of every sentence was
  not done.
