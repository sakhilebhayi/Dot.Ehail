# R-46 decisions log

Append-only. `D-n (agent, date): decision. Why.` Keep both sides on merge conflicts.

D-1 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): Real-time is short polling with versioned deltas; broadcasting events stay for hosts that can run Reverb. Why: the shared cPanel host cannot run a WebSocket server (live /up/realtime is "degraded").
D-2 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): Dispatch advances on demand (every poll and the per-minute scheduler call Dispatcher::tick). Why: no daemons on the host; offers must still expire within seconds.
D-3 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): Money is integer minor units with ISO currency, versioned pricing rule sets, server-authoritative quotes with expiry. Why: brief section 6 pricing integrity.
D-4 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): Route-based fares require a real road route from a configured routing provider; straight-line distance is never billed. Why: brief section 10.
D-5 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): Turn-by-turn navigation hands off to the device's maps app; SOS creates an incident, notifies contacts and ops, offers SA emergency numbers; no claim of emergency dispatch. Why: honesty rules, no provider contracts.

D-600 (money, 2026-11-10): The payments table is the existing `ride_payments`, extended additively (purpose, attempt, idempotency_key, payment_method_id, channel, refunded_minor, failure fields, next_retry_at, verified_at, confirmed_by); `App\Domain\Payments\Models\Payment` maps it and `App\Models\RidePayment` keeps working for the legacy checkout. Why: no row migration, legacy rows keep their meaning, one source of truth for every payment attempt.
D-601 (money, 2026-11-10): A payment is recorded as paid only from Paystack's verify answer (matching reference, amount, currency), never from a webhook body or a charge response alone; the legacy webhook path now verifies too. Why: brief section on webhooks; a signed body is a pointer, not proof. Existing webhook tests were migrated to fake the verify endpoint (behaviour change, stated in the audit).
D-602 (money, 2026-11-10): Webhooks are stored once (payment_webhook_events, key = sha256 of event + object id/reference/status/codes, personal data stripped) and processed by a queued job; storing is the acknowledgement. Why: Paystack sends no event id; the host has no daemon, the cron-driven queue and money:sweep retry failures.
D-603 (money, 2026-11-10): Adding a card = card-only Paystack checkout of EHAIL_CARD_SETUP_AMOUNT_MINOR (R1.00), save the authorisation only if Paystack marks it reusable, refund the charge at once. Why: Paystack's public API has no tokenise-without-charge or pre-auth/capture for cards (OpenAPI checked).
D-604 (money, 2026-11-10): The contract's `authorise` checks that a chargeable method exists (no hold); `capture` charges after the trip. Why: no pre-authorisation in Paystack's public API; the post-trip decline risk is handled by retries, the payment-due block and the exceptions list.
D-605 (money, 2026-11-10): Retry policy 15 min, 2 h, 24 h (EHAIL_CHARGE_RETRY_MINUTES); after the last retry the passenger is blocked from new requests (PaymentDueGuard, middleware `ehail.payment-clear`, 402) until settled; unpaid checkout rides block after a 15-minute grace. Why: brief "payment_due state blocking new requests".
D-606 (money, 2026-11-10): Revenue is recognised at trip completion (receivable), settlement is a second journal (gateway clearing / cash with driver / business receivable). Driver earnings are pending until the payment is settled or waived, then available. Why: one uniform recognition rule for every method; drivers are paid for written-off fares; unpaid fares are visible as receivables.
D-607 (money, 2026-11-10): Cash: off by default, category-limited, driver opt-in; the driver's confirmed amount (up to the fare) is what the ledger records; a shortfall opens a finance issue and a support seam, the passenger is not blocked; an overpayment is the driver's. Why: SA cash-robbery risk for drivers; no one is punished on another party's unverified word.
D-608 (money, 2026-11-10): Cash commission is netted at payout: cash_with_driver balances (not already reserved by an open payout) are offset against available earnings; a fully netted payout is settled without a bank movement. Why: brief "commission becomes driver_receivable netted against payouts".
D-609 (money, 2026-11-10): Payout batches are drafted automatically (weekly, configurable) but money moves only by operator action: bank CSV export + mark paid, or Paystack transfers when enabled. Minimum payout R50 default; below minimum or no bank account = carried forward with the reason recorded. Why: honesty, shared-host constraints, owner control of cash out.
D-610 (money, 2026-11-10): Paystack Transfers adapter is OFF by default (EHAIL_PAYSTACK_TRANSFERS) and a transfer is paid only when transfer/verify says success. Why: Paystack transfers need a Registered Business, a funded balance and OTP disabled; the owner must enable it.
D-611 (money, 2026-11-10): ZA bank verification uses Paystack POST /bank/validate (not /bank/resolve, which is NG/GH); the ID/registration number is sent once and never stored; without Paystack the account is "unavailable" for the operator to check. Why: POPIA minimisation, real ZA support.
D-612 (money, 2026-11-10): Ledger immutability enforced in three layers: model guards, DB triggers (SQLite, PostgreSQL; MySQL attempted), and on PostgreSQL a deferred constraint trigger refusing unbalanced journals at commit. Why: brief "DB-level check where possible".
D-613 (money, 2026-11-10): Receipts are HTML (print-friendly, A4 print CSS) + email link; no PDF dependency added (none in the repo). VAT line only when EHAIL_VAT_REGISTERED + number are set and the fare carries tax. Why: brief allows print-friendly HTML when no PDF library exists; no new dependency.
D-614 (money, 2026-11-10): No Dot.Finance or Dot.Billing integration: neither exposes a contract for Ehail (Finance's only sales endpoint is Emall-specific). Ehail exports the ledger as CSV. Why: integrate only on a real contract.
D-615 (money, 2026-11-10): Seams to other domains are money-owned interfaces bound with bindIf defaults (FareSource, EarningsSplitSource, CancellationFeeSource, BusinessBilling, PayeeResolver, DriverActivitySource, SupportCaseOpener); stubs of the contract events TripCompleted/RideCancelled live in App\Domain\Rides\Events for dispatch to replace. Why: build now against the contract without editing other owners' files.
D-616 (money, 2026-11-10): Money reacts both to TripCompleted/RideCancelled and to the ride row reaching completed/cancelled (Ride::updated), queueing idempotent jobs after commit. Why: settles rides completed by any path (legacy lifecycle, operators) before and after dispatch emits the events.
D-617 (money, 2026-11-10): Default commission 20% (EHAIL_COMMISSION_PERCENT) is a placeholder setting for the built-in splitter, not a market claim; pricing's commission plans replace it. Why: an earnings split needs a rate; reported SA rates conflict (20-25% + booking fees).
D-618 (money, 2026-11-10): Money API lives under /money/* (and /money/ops/* for operators) as JSON; bootstrap/app.php renders exceptions as JSON for money/* when the client expects JSON (one-line change to the shared file). Why: wave-2 UIs consume JSON; previously only api/* got JSON errors.
D-619 (money, 2026-11-10): Incentive kinds needing data nobody records yet (online hours, acceptance, referrals) are "unmeasurable" and never awarded until DriverActivitySource is bound. Why: never display or pay an incentive as earned before its rules are verifiably met.
