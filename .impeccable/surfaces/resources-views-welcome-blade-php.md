## Direction contract

THESIS: A ride is a line between two stops; Dot.Ehail owns the route line with stops (the logo's disc is the stop, its chevron the travel) and refuses the category's black-and-white minimalism and neon-on-black.

OWN-WORLD: Cool map-paper ground, sign green (South African direction signs) on small marks and pale green tints for areas, road-marking yellow for the stop that matters (pickup, destination, total, emergency calls), Barlow highway lettering with Barlow Condensed display, pill controls with round caps, discs for stops, flat surfaces separated by tone.

STORY: The visitor learns they will see every part of the fare before requesting, that safety is on every ride and its limits are stated, that drivers see earnings before accepting, and that businesses and fleets are served; they sign in to ride or apply to drive.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left half a surface card: bar with logo, section links and Sign in; the tagline as three stops on a green route line (Your journey, Your safety, Your choice, the last stop yellow); the headline "See the fare before you ride" at about 92px condensed; one sentence; the Sign in to ride button; three text links. Right half: the Nelson Mandela Bridge road photograph full height, yellow lane lines running into the city, slow crossfade to two more South African street photographs.
